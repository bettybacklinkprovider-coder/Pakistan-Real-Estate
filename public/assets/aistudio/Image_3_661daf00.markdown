## Brand & Style

This design system targets high-net-worth property acquisitions, private architectural portfolios, and luxury real estate brokerages. The aesthetic reflects an exclusive, nocturnal gallery experience: stately, quietly confident, and meticulously crafted. 

The visual language blends editorial minimalism with refined glassmorphism:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep oceanic navy creates an immersive, cinematic backdrop that elevates architectural imagery and high-value listings.
- **Editorial Distinction:** Pure white high-contrast serif typography delivers timeless prestige, balanced against modern sans-serif functional metadata.
- **Controlled Drama:** A surgical crimson red accent commands focal actions without compromising the understated, midnight environment.
- **Depth:** Luminous glass surfaces, hairline crystal borders, and subtle crimson/cobalt ambient radial glows generate an ethereal sense of architectural space.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around nocturnal density, high optical contrast, and deliberate focal accents:

- **Base Canvas (`#0A1128`):** Deep abyssal navy serving as the foundational viewport canvas, providing infinite negative space.
- **Elevated Surfaces (`#111A3A`):** Midnight blue container tone, applied across interactive modules, search panels, and cards. Can be expressed with alpha variations (`rgba(17, 26, 58, 0.75)`) when glassmorphic transparency is engaged.
- **Accent Primary (`#C8102E`):** Pure crimson red for definitive high-priority interactions, reserve triggers, price tags, and urgent state highlights.
- **Accent Interactive Hover (`#E11D3F`):** Luminous scarlet red used exclusively for interactive hover states, active glows, and focused controls.
- **Headings & Primary Text (`#FFFFFF`):** Unadulterated pure white for maximum legibility and architectural editorial gravitas.
- **Body & Secondary Copy (`#A9B2C9`):** Soft blue-grey providing balanced contrast that mitigates eye fatigue while preserving dark-canvas hierarchy.
- **Structural Borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`):** Ultra-subtle frosted hairline borders outlining components and structural cards.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a dialogue between editorial heritage and contemporary clarity:

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys bespoke hospitality and timeless wealth. Headings utilize deliberate letter-spacing compression to maintain architectural unity across large viewports.
- **Body & Data Metrics (Manrope):** A clean geometric sans chosen for supreme legibility against high-darkness canvases. Provides razor-sharp rendering of floor plans, property specs, square footage, and valuation data.
- **Uppercase Metadata (`label-caps`):** Used strictly for property status tags (e.g., `OFF-MARKET`, `PENTHOUSE`), section badges, and architectural coordinate markers.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column fluid grid system governs desktop and tablet screens, shifting to a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 768px` (4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`)
  - Tablet: `768px - 1199px` (8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`)
  - Desktop: `≥ 1200px` (12 columns, max content width 1440px, `margin-desktop: 4rem`, `gutter-desktop: 2rem`)
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Built on an 8-point base increment. Spacious macro-gaps (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) isolate property presentations, mirroring the negative space of upscale print catalogues and art monographs.
- **Reflow Behavior:** Grid tiers collapse gracefully: 3-column architectural cards collapse to 2 columns on tablet landscape and single full-bleed cards on mobile viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through glassmorphic layering, subtle rim lighting, and luminous radial glows:

- **Background Ambiance:** Beneath the primary canvas, soft radial gradient lights (`radial-gradient(circle at 80% 10%, rgba(200, 16, 46, 0.08) 0%, transparent 60%)` and `radial-gradient(circle at 10% 70%, rgba(17, 26, 58, 0.6) 0%, transparent 70%)`) wash across the viewport, giving life to the deep navy backdrop.
- **Glassmorphic Tiers:**
  - *Tier 1 (Panels & Navbars):* `background: rgba(17, 26, 58, 0.75)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
  - *Tier 2 (Cards & Overlays):* `background: rgba(17, 26, 58, 0.85)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
  - *Tier 3 (Floating Modals & Flyouts):* `background: #111A3A`, `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(2, 6, 18, 0.7), 0 0 24px rgba(200, 16, 46, 0.12)`.
- **Inner Rim Lighting:** Key cards feature an inset hairline highlight (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`) to simulate beveled crystal edges.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates sculptural, generous curves to soften the technicality of real estate listings:

- **Base Cards & Modules:** Styled with large radii between `20px` (`rounded-lg`) and `28px` (`rounded-xl`), creating tactile, stone-carved physical silhouettes.
- **Buttons & Interactive Tags:** Formed with `0.5rem` to `1rem` corner radii for structured precision.
- **Image Frames:** Architectural hero imagery always inherits card corner radii (`20px - 28px`), retaining edge harmony and preventing harsh visual cuts into dark backgrounds.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Crimson `#C8102E` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `border-radius: 12px`, with transition to `#E11D3F` on hover and a soft crimson halo glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(200, 16, 46, 0.4)`).
  - *Secondary Glass:* `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` fill, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, `#FFFFFF` text, shifting to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` on hover.
  - *Text Action:* `#A9B2C9` color, `Playfair Display` or `Manrope`, transitioning to `#FFFFFF` with an active crimson underline indicator.

- **Listing Cards:**
  - Encased in `24px` radius with `rgba(17, 26, 58, 0.8)` background and `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
  - Edge image masking matches top card corner curves.
  - Hover triggers an elevation lift (`transform: translateY(-4px)`), increased border opacity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`), and an ambient crimson warmth (`box-shadow: 0 16px 40px rgba(10, 17, 40, 0.8), 0 0 30px rgba(200, 16, 46, 0.15)`).

- **Form Inputs & Search Fields:**
  - Dark glass containers (`rgba(10, 17, 40, 0.6)`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` and `12px` border radius.
  - `#A9B2C9` placeholder copy; focused inputs shift border color to `#C8102E` with a faint `0 0 0 3px rgba(200, 16, 46, 0.2)` focus ring.

- **Chips & Badges:**
  - Compact glass tags with `label-caps` typography, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` fill, and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` hairline outline.
  - Priority status badges (e.g., "Exclusive") employ `rgba(200, 16, 46, 0.15)` crimson wash with `#FFFFFF` text.

- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Custom `20px` square or circular glass elements. Unchecked: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. Checked: `#C8102E` solid background with sharp white micro-check indicator.

- **Domain-Specific Components:**
  - *Spec Badges:* Horizontal stat strips (Beds, Baths, Sq Ft) partitioned by micro-borders of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
  - *Private Viewing Scheduler:* Elevated Tier 3 glass modal with interactive crimson calendar date selection and subtle radial background glows.
